The Path We've Traveled

2015

The First Spark

Started as a small 4-seater food stall in the vibrant streets of Madurai, armed with nothing but our grandmother's secret heirloom recipes and a burning passion to serve real food.

2019

The Signature Biryani

Introduced our world-famous Mutton Dum Biryani. The aroma took over the city, word of mouth spread like wildfire, and we became an overnight culinary sensation.

2026

Super Empire

Today, we proudly serve over 50,000+ happy customers across multiple premium outlets, preserving the exact same authentic 2015 taste and unmatched hospitality.

The Secret Behind the Taste

The Magic Behind Our Taste

Guntur Chillies

Sourced directly from the farms of Andhra, adding the perfect fiery kick without any artificial food colors.

Hand-Pounded

We reject machine-ground powders. Every spice blend is traditionally hand-pounded daily for maximum aroma.

Farm Fresh Herbs

Plucked fresh every morning. Coriander, mint, and curry leaves that breathe pure life into our signature dishes.

Slow Fire Cooking

Patience is our ultimate ingredient. Dum biryanis and curries are cooked slow on a low fire to lock in every drop of flavor.

Our Master Chefs .
